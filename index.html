Meridian — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Who we are

Meridian ("the app," "we," "us," "our") is a goals-and-habits app with an optional AI coaching feature, operated by CJK Venture LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is handled, the choices you have, and your rights.

Data controller / contact:
CJK Venture LLC
Contact email: cjkventure@gmail.com

If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at the email above.

2. The short version

  • Your habits, goals, subgoals, statistics, and profile are stored on your device — not on our servers. We don't have a copy, and the app does not sync this data across devices.
  • We don't require an account to use the core app. Sign-in (with Apple or Google) is optional.
  • The AI coach is the one feature that sends data off your device. When you chat with the coach, your messages and the relevant goal/habit context are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. This is the only feature that transmits your content externally.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we don't use it to track you across other apps or websites.

The rest of this policy explains each of these in detail.

3. Information the app handles

3.1 Information stored on your device (not collected by us)

The following is created by you and stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. It is not transmitted to us or stored on our servers, and it is not synced between devices:

  • Your goals, subgoals, and their details (titles, notes, timeframes, completion state).
  • Your habits and their completion history, streaks, and statistics.
  • Your profile details you enter in the app (first name, optional last name, profile picture).
  • Your in-app preferences and settings.

Because this data lives on your device, deleting the app removes it.

3.2 Account sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in, you may use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. These providers authenticate you and may share basic profile information (such as your name and email address) with the app per your choices at sign-in. We use this only to establish your account session and, if available, to populate your profile name. Signing in is optional — you can use the app without an account. Signing out disconnects the account but keeps your local data on the device.

We do not receive or store your Apple or Google password. Those credentials are handled entirely by Apple and Google. If you use Sign in with Apple, you may choose Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, in which case we only ever see a private relay address.

3.3 AI coach conversations

When you use the AI coach, the following is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic, which operates the Claude API) through our secure server (proxy) in order to generate the coach's responses:

  • The messages you send to the coach.
  • Relevant context needed to respond helpfully — such as the goals and habits you are discussing.

This is the only feature in Meridian that transmits your content off your device. It happens only when you actively use the coach. Your full local dataset, your account credentials, and unrelated app data are not sent.

Our server (proxy) passes your coach messages to Anthropic and returns the response to your device. We do not use your coach conversations to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell them. Our proxy processes coach messages transiently to relay them to Anthropic and does not retain the contents of your conversations.

Anthropic processes this data as our service provider to generate coach responses. Anthropic's handling of API data is governed by its own commercial terms and privacy commitments; see Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy for details on how it processes API data.

Please do not enter information you consider highly sensitive (for example, government identifiers, financial account numbers, or health details) into the coach chat, as those messages are transmitted to our AI provider to generate a response.

The AI coach includes reasonable fair-use limits on how much it can be used within a given period. If you reach a usage limit, the coach will let you know and when it will become available again. These limits help us keep the feature reliable and sustainable for everyone.

3.4 Subscription and purchase information

The AI coach is a paid feature. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Apple handles all payment processing. We receive from Apple only the information needed to know whether your subscription is active (your entitlement status).

3.5 Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device for habit reminders. These are generated and delivered on-device and are not sent through or stored on our servers.

3.6 Information we do not collect

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos (beyond a profile picture you deliberately choose, which stays on your device), advertising identifiers, or browsing history. Meridian does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking SDKs, and we do not perform tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

4. How we use information

  • To run the app's features — your on-device data powers your goals, habits, statistics, and reminders, all locally.
  • To provide the AI coach — your coach messages and relevant context are sent to Anthropic to generate responses, only when you use the coach.
  • To authenticate you — if you choose to sign in, to establish your session.
  • To manage subscriptions — to determine whether you have access to the paid coach feature.
  • To send notifications — if you enable them, for habit reminders, scheduled locally on your device.
  • To maintain security and integrity — to protect the app and our proxy from abuse, and to operate the service reliably.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the app and, if you subscribe, the AI coach.
  • Consent — for optional features you choose to enable, such as sign-in, notifications, or using the coach. You may withdraw consent at any time by not using the feature, signing out, or disabling notifications.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the app and our proxy secure and functioning, in a way that does not override your rights.

7. International data transfers

Our AI provider, proxy host, and sign-in providers may process data on servers located in the United States or other countries. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with data-transfer restrictions, this means your coach messages and sign-in data may be transferred internationally when you use those features. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) provided by these service providers.

8. Data retention

  • On-device data (habits, goals, stats, profile) remains on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Coach messages are processed to generate responses. Our proxy does not retain the contents of your coach conversations; they are processed only in transit to generate a response. Anthropic's retention of API data is governed by its terms; see its privacy policy.
  • Subscription status is maintained by Apple for the life of your subscription.
  • We retain any support correspondence you send us (for example, emails to our contact address) only as long as needed to address your request.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK) or the CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

  • Your on-device data: you can view, edit, and delete it directly in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes it entirely.
  • The AI coach: it is optional — you control what you send it, and you can choose not to use it.
  • Sign-in: optional — you can use the app without an account and can sign out at any time.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. We also do not process sensitive personal information for the purpose of inferring characteristics about you.
  • Exercising your rights: to make a privacy request or ask a question, contact us at cjkventure@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law, and we will not discriminate against you for making a request. For data stored only on your device, the fastest way to delete it is within the app or by deleting the app. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Children's privacy

Meridian is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The AI coach is intended for a general adult audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it and delete such information.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the app. Coach messages are sent to our provider over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and API credentials are held securely on our server and are never included in the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take steps to protect your information. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and authorities as required by applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes or as required by law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Your continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
