Frequently asked questions

How do I create a goal or habit?

On the Goals or Habits tab, use the add button to create a new one. Goals can include subgoals to break a big ambition into steps, and habits can be scheduled with reminders.

What is the AI coach, and is it free?

The AI coach is a conversational guide that helps you think through your goals, stay accountable, and work through challenges. The coach is a paid feature offered through an auto-renewing subscription. The rest of Meridian — goals, subgoals, habits, and statistics — is free to use.

Do I need an account to use Meridian?

No. You can use the full core app without signing in. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and simply lets you associate the app with your account.

Is my data private?

Your habits, goals, and statistics are stored locally on your device — not on our servers. The one exception is the AI coach: when you chat with it, your messages are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Does my data sync across devices?

Not currently. Meridian stores your data on the device you're using, so your goals and habits live on that device. Cross-device sync is something we may add in a future update.

How do I export or delete my data?

In Settings, you'll find options to download your data and to delete all of your data. Deleting the app also removes all locally stored data from your device.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled through your Apple ID. To view, change, or cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Meridian. You can also manage subscriptions in the App Store app under your account.

The coach says it needs a connection.

The AI coach requires an internet connection to generate responses. The rest of the app (goals, habits, statistics) works offline; only the coach needs to be online.

Is there a limit on how much I can use the coach?

Yes. The coach has generous fair-use limits over a given period so we can keep it reliable and sustainable for everyone. Normal use will not run into them. If you ever reach a limit, the coach will tell you and let you know when it becomes available again. The rest of the app stays fully usable in the meantime.

How should I think about the coach's advice?

The coach is an AI assistant meant to offer encouragement, ideas, and general guidance for your goals and habits. It is not a professional, and its responses may not always be accurate or right for your situation. Use your own judgment, and for medical, legal, financial, or other professional matters, please consult a qualified professional. See our Terms of Service for details.
